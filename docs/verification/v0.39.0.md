## Limits

Receipt hashes bind declared observations to their full fixture, evidence,
configuration and local source closure; they do not authenticate inference,
provider identity, origin, truth or permission. Temperature zero does not prove
determinism. Provider usage may be absent and billing remains unmeasured.
Stage metrics are separate from strict four-phase selective repair; this batch
makes no unsupported selective-repair or general security-efficacy claim.

Public artifacts contain source and sanitized verification only. Private logs,
company data, model paths and credentials are excluded. No real vault, external
corpus import, automatic memory-policy deployment, official SDK dependency or
default model/network call is added. Actual inference remains explicit opt-in
through the bounded literal-loopback transport.
