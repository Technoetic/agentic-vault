## Boundaries

Formation is a deterministic synthetic functional evaluation. It does not run a
model, reproduce official MemForm results or establish general injection resistance;
the bounded policy still loses the legitimate task in two poisoned cases. Host-
reported own origin and current provenance do not authenticate factual truth.

Assembly separates host-selected structured extraction from explicit source policy.
It requires scope/polarity and otherwise abstains without migrating existing ledgers.
Literal identities, positive polarity, confirmed status and half-open validity do not
provide general semantic entailment or authority inference. Conflicting authorized
current values abstain; a more recent timestamp alone cannot override institution policy.

Procedural recall returns candidates for review with source substrings,
execution-order DAGs and compatible current native host observations. These do not
certify human review, satisfied preconditions, truth or execution of the full returned
procedure. Procedure execution edges are distinct from improvement-candidate ancestry.
Steps are never executed or promoted by lookup.

No default hook/search/model/network path or runtime dependency is added. Results
are advisory and do not apply real-vault changes. Formation's explicitly approved
ADD/EDIT operations are confined to disposable synthetic vaults. Source/policy
rechecks are point-in-time observations rather than an atomic multi-file snapshot.
New Linux/macOS runtime observations are pending at preparation time and must be
recorded from final CI in `verification.json`. Actual business effectiveness and total
costs remain unmeasured.
