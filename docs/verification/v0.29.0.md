## Release preparation and final gates

Version surfaces and the version-only healthcheck stamp/`ENGINE_VERSION` target 0.29.0.
Existing default session/recall behavior, 15 slash commands, the warning checker's
seven files and harmless corpus remain unchanged from v0.28.0; content-identical rule
stamps remain as before. `commands/vault-session-end.md` gains one optional sentence.
Documentation that described the three features as source-only now names v0.29.0.

The final gates below are recorded in the pre-publication `verification.json` for the
fixed source commit, not in this file:

- metadata, README truth and Codex verification tests; `claude plugin validate .` and
  `claude plugin validate .claude-plugin/plugin.json`; a disposable Codex installation;
- default-output comparisons against v0.28.0 without the new flags;
- master and tag CI on the final commit;
- the ZIP built with `TZ=UTC0` and prefix `agentic-vault-0.29.0/`, its `rebuild_spec`,
  L1 byte and L2 member/blob checks;
- an independent read-only review of the frozen receipt and package.

Release attestation and anonymous re-download checks happen after publication and are
kept outside the immutable assets in a separate `post-publication.json`.

## Limits

- Answer rates and lower bounds describe the chosen evaluation results. Fixtures and
  deterministic controls do not measure real model or business performance.
- Lower bounds assume independent queries that share one rate and carry no multiplicity
  adjustment across summaries.
- `preserve-check` counts occurrences without positions: swaps or masking additions are
  not detected, rewording can look like a removal, and literal equality is not semantic
  preservation. Results never gate, apply or approve a proposal.
