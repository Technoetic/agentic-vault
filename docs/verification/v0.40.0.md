## Limits

Routing-control success is not actual host selection, correct skill execution
or business success. A complete empty observation can establish a negative pass;
missing, unavailable or error observations cannot. Invocation, file-read and
self-report evidence remain distinct. Repeated valid calls are not automatically
penalized, and a neutral helper cannot hide missing composition or forbidden calls.

Hashes bind supplied artifacts and grader source, not identity, truth, permission
or authentic host events. Comparison requires trusted grader outputs and validates
internal consistency; it cannot regrade omitted expectations or authenticate
caller-authored grades. Recomputed hashes cannot turn altered grades into trusted
results. Unsupported or incompatible evidence cannot produce an improvement.

The manifest parser is a strict bounded subset, not general YAML. Catalog
overlap is advisory and does not measure model confusion. No third-party code,
prompts or dataset is copied, and no private company data or credentials enter
the public fixtures. Evaluation adds no model/network/subprocess calls, installed
skill, hook, SKILL-description or configuration-default changes. Publication,
active installation updates and actual vault changes remain separate operations.
